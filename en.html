Our Expertise

Metal Surface Pretreatment (MPT)

Comprehensive solutions for industrial surface preparation ensuring quality, durability, and performance.

Aluminum Pretreatment

Surface preparation process used before painting, powder coating, anodizing, or adhesive bonding. The goal is to remove contaminants, improve corrosion resistance, and enhance coating adhesion.

Key Processes
  • Cleaning / Degreasing — removes oils, grease, and dirt.
  • Acid Etching — dissolves thin layer, improves roughness.
  • Conversion Coating — chemically bonded layer for corrosion resistance.
Metal Coils Pretreatment

Continuous, automated surface preparation process applied to steel or aluminum coils before they are coil-coated with paint or film. Ensures strong adhesion and corrosion resistance.
